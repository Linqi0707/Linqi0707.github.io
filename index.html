About me

My name is Qingyao Zhang, and I am currently pursuing a master's degree in Electronic Information at the Institute of Electronic Science and Technology, University of Electronic Science and Technology of China (UESTC). I received my bachelor's degree in Computer Science and Technology (Big Data track) from China University of Geosciences (Wuhan).

My research focuses on AI-based radar target detection algorithms. I am interested in exploring how artificial intelligence can improve target detection in radar signals.
